# Problem & Goals

## Problem (one sentence)
Voyagers, GalapagosIslands.travel and Polar Cruises & Tours produce expedition-travel
content reactively, so high-intent search/AI-visibility opportunities are found late
(or missed) and briefing a writer is a manual, ad-hoc research task.

## Goals (measurable)
- G1: Surface corroborated, on-brand, on-score trends automatically, daily.
- G2: Cut "rising → brief in hand" to under 1 day for weekly briefing runs.
- G3: Every escalated trend carries a defensible rationale and a multichannel brief.
- G4: Zero off-brand trends reach a brief (luxury/brand-fit gate).
